Poem / 04

Course Solomon

-Debolina Tapadar

Find me in another timeline,
And I may look at you the same way,
The way that day the shadow
passed through my shoulder.
The way I passed by never looking back.

I may not know who I was,
Or may be I was just a pandora
Of an overwhelming set of true emotions.
I was me when I stayed,
But something changed when I left.

Just the fact I don't see the world
As what it could be,
But just how solemnly it is
Crowded, silent and shattered.

Poem / 06

Lost

~DEBOLINA TAPADAR

Oh what have I lost?
And what did I gain?
Life just slipping through my fingers
And I just standing in vain.

Some dear ones lost in the wheel of time,
And some just erased,
Like they never existed in the timeline.

Have I grown too old now?
Why do I have to bear the sorrow?
Yet I still in my dreams find myself wandering,
In the same stitched cotton white dress,
In the same anklets and bangles,
Wobbling in my small feet,
In the old smell of maa's home.

Can the dream be longer?
And yet I opened my eyes again
And I am again old enough to be alone now.

Poem / 07

The Street Light

~Debolina Tapadar

Walking pass the lane,
I saw him be a bit dimmer from far,
And then closer near it lost its light.
Just a darker spot on a well lit road.

Aren't u lonely I asked,
In a street full of lights u share a darker spot,
He replied "I am not broken u see,
Just a bit tired,
Not the darkest but a bit gloomy to be honest.
They haven't replaced me, so may be I am happier at best"

I asked "But aren't u lonely?"
Well he replied "are we all not lonely in this chaos?"
I nodded at best.
He asked "Do u like the weather?"
I said "The chilling the weather the calmer I feel"

Isn’t it odd he asked.
"Isn’t it?"
I started walking again,
Turned back after a distance,
He started glowing once again
May be just brighter than the other on the lane.
I smiled and started walking along.

Poem / 12

Me

Why did she remain as a stain,
Rather than a story,
How dark were those hallows,
Where even the hand reached out for help,
Seemed dark.

Why was always there a pause,
Before those wishes.
May be it didn't mean to be as loud,
Or she was just not enough.

Weighing by the tongs,
It was just thrown and betrayed.
As may be it wasn't her story.

Oh to be delusional was a hobby,
May be being avoided was a bliss.

Lets just break ...
Till she cant reply,
Till her bone breaks and her head bleeds,
And dies through every breaking moment
The unloved undeserving ungrateful birth
